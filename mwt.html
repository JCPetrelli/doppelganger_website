MWT

Projection mapping — video shaped onto architecture and objects.

About

MWT (m-w-t.berlin) is where Doppelganger — Jacopo Castellano and Niels Poensgen — first built large-surface work together, in 2023, before the duo settled into its live club practice.

The premise is projection mapping: footage cut and warped to fit a specific structure, thrown onto walls and objects so the image belongs to the surface rather than to a rectangular screen. It is the same instinct that runs through everything the duo does since — the picture living on real architecture, in a real room — worked out here at the scale of a building. Two pieces remain from it.
